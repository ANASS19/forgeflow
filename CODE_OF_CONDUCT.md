# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Technical disagreement is welcome;
harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, and publishing another person's
private information are not.

Keep discussions relevant and constructive. Explain reproducible problems,
assume good intent, and credit others' work accurately. Do not post credentials,
private execution data, or confidential code.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions,
or restrict participation to protect the project. For a conduct concern, ask a
maintainer for a private reporting channel without reposting sensitive material.
